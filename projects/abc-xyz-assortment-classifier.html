Problem Definition
Understanding ABC and XYZ logic

ABC classifies SKUs by cumulative revenue share. XYZ adds volatility using coefficient of variation on weekly sales. Together they create actionable groups such as AX, BY, and CZ.

2
Data Preparation
Reshaping weekly sales data

Loaded a multi-week sales CSV, aggregated by SKU and week, pivoted the data into a wide format, and handled missing weeks as zero-sales where appropriate.

3
ABC Classification
Calculating cumulative revenue share

Sorted SKUs by total revenue, calculated cumulative percentage, and assigned A, B, or C labels using the 70/90/100 breakpoints.

4
XYZ Classification
Measuring demand stability

Calculated coefficient of variation for every SKU and bucketed products into X, Y, and Z groups based on volatility.

5
Export
Building a formatted Excel output

Used openpyxl to export the classification table with conditional formatting and a summary sheet for counts and revenue by class.
