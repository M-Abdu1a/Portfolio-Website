Power BI · Hospitality Analytics

AtliQ Hospitality Dashboard

A Power BI report for AtliQ Grand that compares bookings, revenue, occupancy, and cancellations across properties, room types, channels, and dates.

Back to projects

Project breakdown

1
Business Context
Defining the hotel performance questions

The project focused on declining market share and revenue loss for AtliQ Grand. The report was designed to show where revenue was leaking, which properties were underperforming, and how booking behaviour changed across channels and time periods.

2
Power Query
Preparing booking and revenue data for analysis

Cleaned and shaped the raw booking tables, standardised dates and categorical fields, and prepared the bookings, aggregated bookings, rooms, hotels, and date tables for a consistent reporting model.

3
Data Model
Connecting the hotel data tables

Built a star schema with fact bookings and aggregated bookings connected to hotel, room, and date dimensions. Checked relationship direction and filter behaviour so the measures remained reliable across property, city, room class, platform, and weekday selections.

4
DAX
Writing the hotel performance measures

Created measures for Revenue, RevPAR, DSRN, DURN, ADR, Occupancy %, Realized Revenue, Cancellation Rate, Realized Booking Rate, and week-over-week changes. These measures turn operational booking data into metrics leadership can act on.

5
Report Design
Assembling the Power BI report

Designed an interactive report with KPI cards, revenue by category, metric trends, property-level detail, and weekday versus weekend comparisons. Filters allow users to move from a portfolio-level view to a specific city, property, room class, platform, or date range.

6
Business Insights
Comparing properties, channels, and room categories

The analysis highlighted revenue leakage across third-party booking platforms versus direct channels, weekday and weekend occupancy patterns, and underperforming properties and room categories that could inform pricing and channel strategy.
